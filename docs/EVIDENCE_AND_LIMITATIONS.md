## Not established by this repository

The original machine's exact center of mass, weight, wheel contact-force distribution, overhang, acceleration trajectory, structural safety, hydraulic ratings, exact historic tipping threshold or motor-startup root cause. No field-tested design improvement or independent experimental validation is claimed.

The small-stone CAD depiction is a **design view**. It is not a test photograph or proof of the actual suspended stone's measured location. The graph is **hypothetical analysis**, not experimental wheel-force data.

## Verification and preservation

Numerical tests check arithmetic consistency only, not correspondence to the physical machine. Retain older source files, even those carrying superseded commentary, in private archival storage. Do not change timestamps, invent tests, or conflate design CAD with as-built measurements. If a school asks how software and external assistance were used, respond according to the actual workflow.

The included documents are for educational and portfolio review, not for construction or operation of lifting equipment.
